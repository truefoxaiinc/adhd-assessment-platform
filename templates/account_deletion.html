Account deletion

Delete Your Attention Minder Account

This page explains how Attention Minder users can request account deletion and what happens to associated data.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Attention Minder app.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Profile or Settings.
  4. Choose Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

The app uses the logged-in account to submit the deletion request. Do not share your password or token with anyone.

If you cannot access the app

Send an email from your registered email address and request account deletion.

Email: truefoxaiinc.official@gmail.com

Data deleted or deactivated

  • Your account is deactivated and can no longer be used to sign in.
  • Your account is marked as deleted in our backend systems.
  • Assessment responses, progress records, goals, and attention session records associated with your account are no longer used for active app access.

Data that may be retained

Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, payment records, audit logs, backup retention, or dispute handling. Retained records are kept only for the period required for those purposes.

Processing time

In-app deletion is processed immediately for account access. Email requests are reviewed and processed within 30 days after we verify account ownership.

Public URL for Google Play

Use this URL in Google Play Console: https://attention.truefoxaiinc.com/account-deletion/
